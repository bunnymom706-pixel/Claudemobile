# Resources

Free sources behind the skills in `.claude/skills/`. Everything here is free to
read or free to start; paid items are marked.

## Prompting — official and free

- [Anthropic Interactive Prompt Engineering Tutorial](https://github.com/anthropics/prompt-eng-interactive-tutorial)
  — 9 chapters plus appendix, exercises, answer key. The best single free
  resource for learning to write prompts rather than collect them.
- [Claude Cookbooks](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks) — 44k+
  stars. Runnable notebooks: embeddings, citations, structured outputs, computer
  use, MCP.
- [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills) — Apache 2.0 skills.
  Install with `/plugin marketplace add anthropics/skills`.
- [langgptai/awesome-claude-prompts](https://github.com/langgptai/awesome-claude-prompts)
  — curated community collection.
- [Piebald-AI/claude-code-system-prompts](https://github.com/Piebald-AI/claude-code-system-prompts)
  — Claude Code's own system prompt and tool descriptions. Instructive if you
  want to see how a production agent is prompted.
- [promptingguide.ai](https://www.promptingguide.ai/) — DAIR.AI, comprehensive.

## Software design

- **John Ousterhout, *A Philosophy of Software Design*** — ~190 pages. Read this
  first; highest leverage on the list. Complexity, deep modules, change
  amplification. (Paid, short, worth it.)
- [Fowler's refactoring catalog](https://refactoring.com/catalog/) — free
  online. A reference, not a cover-to-cover read.
- **Michael Feathers, *Working Effectively with Legacy Code*** — read only when
  you hit untested code you are afraid to change. (Paid.)
- [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/) — free, technical, never sells
  anything.

## Money

- **Morgan Housel** — *The Psychology of Money*; free archive at Collab Fund.
- **Nick Maggiulli** — *Of Dollars and Data*, free blog, data-driven.
- **Ramit Sethi** — free content on automation and systems.
- 2026 reference figures: IRA contribution limit **$7,500**; 401(k) limit
  **$24,500**; Roth single-filer full-contribution MAGI under **$153,000**.
  Verify against [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500)
  before acting — these change annually.

## Training and nutrition

- **Layne Norton, PhD** (Nutritional Sciences, Illinois) — free myth-debunking,
  adherence and flexible dieting.
- **Stronger by Science** (Greg Nuckols, Eric Trexler) — free articles and
  podcast, reads the actual literature.
- **Renaissance Periodization** (Mike Israetel) — free YouTube, practical diet
  and hypertrophy.
- **Peter Attia** — longevity and metabolic health; partly free.
- Caveat: Huberman is enormously popular but has drawn credible criticism for
  overstating findings. Cross-check against the above.
- On RED-S / low energy availability: [Center for Young Women's Health](https://youngwomenshealth.org/guides/female-athlete-triad/)
  and [Gatorade Sports Science Institute on female bone health](https://www.gssiweb.org/sports-science-exchange/article/nutrition-for-female-athlete-bone-health).

## Life and work

- **James Clear** — free 3-2-1 newsletter, habits.
- **Cal Newport** — free blog and podcast; deep work, anti-hustle.
- **Oliver Burkeman** — *Four Thousand Weeks*. The counterweight when
  optimization tips into anxiety.
- **BJ Fogg** — Tiny Habits, the behavior-design research underneath Clear.

## Creator economy — read with care

- **Dan Koe** — [free Substack](https://letters.thedankoe.com/), two letters a
  week; [AI and prompts archive](https://letters.thedankoe.com/t/ai-and-prompts).
  Courses are paid; the prompts are not.
- **Nicolas Cole & Dickie Bush** — Write With AI, the leading paid newsletter
  for Claude/ChatGPT prompts.
- **Ruben Hassid** — [How to AI](https://ruben.substack.com/), tactical.
- **Justin Welsh** — [Saturday Solopreneur](https://www.justinwelsh.me/), free.
- **Naval Ravikant** — *Almanack*, free at navalmanack.com. The source much of
  this genre paraphrases.
- **Alex Hormozi** — free YouTube; *$100M Offers* / *$100M Leads*.
- **Sahil Bloom** — free newsletter; the 5 Types of Wealth frame.
- **Ethan Mollick** — [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/), free,
  research-grounded. His [work](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5165270)
  argues prompt engineering is contingent and fading — the useful corrective to
  anyone selling a prompt pack.

Tells for the resale layer: Gumroad and Skool links, "30,000+ prompts", "8
insane prompts", a year in the title. Most of it repackages the free primary
sources above.

## Also worth knowing

- **Price Pritchett, *you²*** — 18 principles, short. Try differently rather
  than harder; solutions are easier than problems; move before you are ready.
  Underpins `/debug-reset` and `/quantum-leap`. (Paid, ~$20. Pirated PDFs
  circulate; the book is short and cheap.)
