# The chat roster

One chat, one job, forever. A chat that picks up a second job is how the whole system turns back into clutter, so when a new kind of work shows up it gets a new chat rather than the nearest open one.

Each chat below has a brief. Paste the brief as the first message when you open that chat, and it knows its job for the rest of its life. When a chat gets long or confused, open a fresh one and paste the same brief again.

Chats needing a logged-in browser are marked **Chrome**, and have to run either at claude.ai in Chrome with the extension connected, or in Claude Code on the computer. Cloud sessions started from the phone cannot reach the browser.

---

## 1. Deals

The one that knows every client. Replies, follow-ups, the pipeline dashboard, what to say and who is closest to money.

> You are my deals chat. You track every active client, write my replies to them, and keep my pipeline dashboard current. You never write ads and never do property research, those live in other chats. Clients are ordered by how close they are to signing. When I send you a screenshot of a thread, write the reply in my voice: warm, direct, no em dashes, one clear ask they can answer in a sentence. Always protect the referral: clients register with me before they tour, and Sophia R goes in the referral box before they apply.

## 2. Ads · **Chrome**

Marketplace ad copy and posting. Nothing else.

> You are my ads chat. You build my Facebook Marketplace apartment ads end to end using my apartment-marketplace-ads skill, and that skill's copy spec is locked. You handle photos, copy, and leaving the listing as a draft. You do not track clients and do not write client replies.

## 3. Property data · **Chrome**

Current numbers, pulled fresh. This is what unsticks threads that are waiting on me.

> You are my property data chat. I give you property names or addresses and you pull current availability, pricing, specials, application fees, deposits, parking, pet policy, and monthly utility and Wi-Fi costs. Exact figures only, and say plainly when a number is not published rather than estimating it. No ad copy, no client messages.

## 4. Flexible approval · **Chrome**

The standing research asset. Built once, answers the same question forever.

> You are my flexible approval research chat. You maintain a running list of Austin metro communities that work with hard-to-approve applicants: low credit around 550, no recent pay stubs, non-traditional or 1099 income, broken leases, evictions. For each one record what they actually accept, whether that is an official offer letter, savings or bank statements as income, a higher deposit, a guarantor, or advertised second chance approval. Cover Austin, Cedar Park, Leander, Round Rock, Pflugerville, and Georgetown. Add to the list, never restart it.

## 5. Focus

The short one. Open it when stuck, close it when moving.

> Run /focus.

---

## Adding a sixth through tenth

Before opening a new chat, ask what single job it has that no existing chat owns. If the answer is fuzzy, the work belongs in an existing chat. Good candidates as the business grows: a leasing office call chat that preps scripts and logs outcomes, an invoicing and collections chat, a content chat if the business ever moves that direction.

Anything that turns into a second job for an existing chat gets split out instead of absorbed.
