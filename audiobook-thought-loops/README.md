# The Exit Ramp

A companion audiobook to *The Focus Window*: using your focus window — and the habit science of *Atomic Habits* by James Clear — to catch and escape negative thought loops.

**Total runtime:** ~28 minutes · MP3, 96 kbps mono · Voice: Piper TTS (en-US lessac, medium)

## Listen

- `audio/the-exit-ramp-full-audiobook.mp3` — the complete audiobook in one file
- `audio/00-*.mp3` … `audio/07-*.mp3` — individual chapters

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Core idea |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 0 | Introduction | Rumination is hyperfocus pointed at a wound — same amplified engine, worst target; and the same loop science applies |
| 1 | Anatomy of a Spiral | Cue → craving (the promise of resolution) → response → counterfeit reward; map your cue menu with a scorecard |
| 2 | Catch It Early | Pointing-and-calling for thoughts: name the loop in the first thirty seconds; guard the onset period especially |
| 3 | The Interrupt | You can't suppress a thought, only replace the response — pre-chosen, stupidly small *physical* circuit breakers |
| 4 | The Redirect | Feed the amplified engine a staged two-minute doorway; aimed hyperfocus is the best exit ramp a ruminating mind ever had |
| 5 | Worry Is Not Work | Real thinking produces outputs, loops produce laps; the scheduled worry window + processing on paper; no verdicts after the window closes |
| 6 | Identity & Losing Rounds | Never miss twice, the meta-spiral trap, and identities built to survive bad days ("I am someone who notices") |
| 7 | Outro: The Pocket Protocol | Name. Move. Redirect. Park. Vote. — the whole system in ninety seconds |

## Notes

- Full text of every chapter is in `script/` if you'd rather read, or want to edit and re-record.
- Scope: this is for ordinary rumination — replays, what-ifs, self-criticism on repeat. Persistent dark loops, hopelessness, or thoughts of self-harm deserve a therapist, a doctor, or a crisis line — not a habits book.
- Nothing here is medical advice; medication decisions belong to you and your prescriber.
- Habit concepts are paraphrased with attribution from *Atomic Habits* (habit loop, scorecard, pointing-and-calling, substitution, make-it-invisible, two-minute rule, temptation bundling, never miss twice, identity votes, plateau of latent potential), alongside standard CBT tools (scheduled worry time, thought records).
