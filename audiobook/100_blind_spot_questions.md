# 100 Blind-Spot Questions for Sophia

Answer fast and honest — gut answers beat perfect ones. "I don't know" is a valid answer, and every "I don't know" IS a blind spot: circle it. Do them in batches of 10 so it doesn't fry your focus. Voice-memo your answers if typing feels like a wall.

## Part 1 — Your Money Mechanics (the leaks are usually here)

1. What is your exact commission split with One Place Locators — the real number, not a guess?
2. What was your average net commission per lease over your last 10 closes?
3. How many properties currently owe you an unpaid invoice, and for how much total?
4. When a property is slow to pay, what is your follow-up process — or do you just wait?
5. What did you gross in your best month ever, and what specifically made that month different?
6. Are you setting aside money for quarterly taxes right now, yes or no?
7. Do you know what you can legally deduct as a 1099 agent (mileage, phone, marketing), and are you tracking any of it?
8. How much is in your emergency fund today, to the dollar?
9. How much do you spend monthly, roughly — could you write the number down without checking?
10. If you closed zero leases next month, how many weeks could you cover your bills?

## Part 2 — Your Pipeline (where the money actually comes from)

11. Where did each of this month's ~4 closes originally come from — can you name the source of every single one?
12. How many NEW lead conversations did you start last week — the actual count?
13. What's your average response time when a new lead messages you?
14. How many leads from the past 90 days went cold without a final follow-up from you?
15. Do you have a written follow-up sequence, or do you follow up when you happen to remember?
16. How many of your closed clients ever gave you a Google review?
17. How many ever gave you a referral — and did you ask, or did it just happen?
18. What percentage of your serious clients do you escort vs. just send lists to?
19. Do you know your close rate — of every 10 real leads, how many sign?
20. If your leads doubled tomorrow, would your system handle it, or would people fall through cracks?

## Part 3 — Your Market Knowledge (the locator who knows most, earns most)

21. Can you name 10 Austin properties right now paying 100%+ commission?
22. Can you name 5 properties offering 2+ months free right now?
23. Which lease-ups opened in your area in the last 6 months — have you toured them?
24. Do you know which properties pay MORE for escorted visits?
25. Which neighborhoods do you actually know street-by-street, and which do you fake?
26. Do you have relationships with any leasing managers who text YOU when specials drop?
27. What do you know about second-chance / broken-lease properties in Austin — could you serve that client tomorrow?
28. Do you know which properties work with travel nurses and corporate relocations?
29. When did you last physically tour a property you weren't showing to a client?
30. If a client asked "why is rent dropping in Austin?", could you explain it in 30 seconds?

## Part 4 — Your Time & Energy (ADHD edition)

31. What hours of the day is your brain actually sharpest — and what do you currently do in those hours?
32. What's your biggest daily time leak, honestly? (Scrolling? Redoing things? Decision paralysis?)
33. How many times a day do you switch tasks before finishing the first one?
34. Do you take your medication consistently as prescribed, or do you skip/forget days?
35. What time did you go to sleep the last 3 nights?
36. What tasks do you avoid so hard that they build up and cause anxiety spikes?
37. When you "work all day," how many of those hours are actual money-producing activity?
38. What would a 30-minute "floor day" routine look like for you — could you write it down right now?
39. Do you use timers/alarms/external reminders, or rely on your memory?
40. What's one thing you keep saying you'll do "when things calm down" — and how long have you been saying it?

## Part 5 — Your Mental Health Foundation (the machine that runs everything)

41. Do you currently have a therapist or counselor, and if not, what's actually stopping you?
42. When your depression spikes, what are your first 3 warning signs — can you name them?
43. What actually helps when anxiety hits — and do you do it, or just know it?
44. Who knows the real state of your mental health — and is that enough people?
45. What does your recovery support look like right now — meetings, sponsor, community, anything?
46. What situations, people, or feelings put your recovery most at risk?
47. When you feel numb, what's the smallest thing that reliably brings you back?
48. Are you using work/hustle as avoidance of feelings sometimes — be honest?
49. What happened right before your last big anxiety/depression spike — is there a pattern?
50. If the "something terrible is coming" feeling had a voice, what exactly does it say — have you ever written it down?

## Part 6 — Your Relationships & Boundaries

51. Does your boyfriend know your actual financial goals and numbers, or just the vibe?
52. Is there any leftover money-guilt with him or your parents from the borrowing days that needs a repair conversation?
53. Who in your life drains you, and how much access do they still have?
54. Who in your life believes in your goals — and do you actually spend time with them?
55. Can you say "no" to plans when you need a recovery night, without spiraling into guilt?
56. Are you helping your parents in ways that quietly sabotage your own stability?
57. Who is your one call when you're about to make a bad decision?
58. Do you have any friendships with women ahead of you in this business?
59. When you're present with your boyfriend, is your phone actually away?
60. Is there anyone you're still trying to prove wrong — and is that fuel or poison?

## Part 7 — Your Identity & Paradigms (Bob Proctor / You² territory)

61. Complete honestly: "People like me don't get to ______."
62. What income number feels 'not for someone like me' — where's your invisible ceiling?
63. Whose voice is that ceiling in — yours, or someone from your past?
64. What did your family teach you about money, spoken or unspoken?
65. Do you secretly believe the good streak will be taken away? Where did that start?
66. What would you attempt this month if failure were literally impossible?
67. What's the boldest thing you've ever done — and what did it prove you're capable of?
68. When someone compliments your success, do you receive it or deflect it?
69. What part of "impressive Sophia" do you already have that you refuse to own?
70. If your inner child watched you work yesterday, what would she be proud of, and what would confuse her?

## Part 8 — Your Fear, Safety & The Existential Stuff

71. What specifically is the "terrible thing" your anxiety predicts — name it in words?
72. Has any version of it actually happened? How did you handle what DID happen?
73. What dollar amount in savings would make you feel genuinely safe — pick a number?
74. When the "it's all pointless" thoughts come — what time of day is it usually, and are you tired, hungry, or alone?
75. Do the existential thoughts show up more when you're moving toward your goals or drifting?
76. What's your evidence from your own life that trying DOES matter? (Hint: you got clean.)
77. What are you doing when you feel most alive and present?
78. What would "feeling in control" actually look like on a random Tuesday — describe it?
79. If you knew everything would be okay, what would you do differently starting tomorrow?
80. What's one fear you've never said out loud to anyone?

## Part 9 — Your Health & The 135 Goal

81. Is your food tracking honest on bad days, or do bad days go unlogged?
82. What derails your eating most — emotions, social situations, or lack of planning?
83. Are you eating enough protein to keep muscle while losing (rough grams per day)?
84. Do you actually hit 10k steps daily, or is it more like 3 days a week?
85. Any strength training? (It's the difference between "smaller" and the body you're picturing.)
86. How's your sleep — hours and quality — and do you see the link between bad sleep and bad brain days?
87. Is the scale a tool for you or a mood-decider?
88. What will you do the week the scale doesn't move — what's the pre-decided plan?
89. Are your Adderall, appetite, and eating patterns something you've discussed with your prescriber?
90. Whose body are you chasing — yours at your healthiest, or someone from Instagram?

## Part 10 — The Leap Itself

91. If you could only do ONE marketing activity for 90 days, which would you bet on — and why aren't you all-in on it already?
92. What's the ONE task that, done daily, would make you unrecognizable in 6 months?
93. What are you currently doing that you should completely STOP doing?
94. Who is the best locator you know of in Austin — and what exactly do they do that you don't?
95. What would you need to believe about yourself to walk into a lease-up and negotiate like you belong?
96. If you hit $10k/month, what's the FIRST thing that money changes — do you know, or is it vague?
97. What does financial freedom actually mean to you in numbers — monthly income, savings, by what age?
98. What part of this plan are you already quietly deciding not to do — and why that part?
99. Six months from now, what will you wish you had started today?
100. What's the first phone call you're going to make tomorrow morning?
