# Active clients

Working format for tracking live deals. The real file is `clients.local.md`, which is gitignored and never committed.

Order clients by how close they are to signing, closest at the top. The point of this file is to never re-read a message thread to remember where a deal stands.

---

## [Name]

**Status:** one line. Qualifying, touring, applying, waiting on approval, or closed.
**Source:** where the lead came from.

Situation in two or three lines. Where they are moving from, budget, timeline, anything that shapes what to send them.

**Hangup:** the one thing standing between them and approval. Credit, income, broken lease, pets, timing. Name it plainly, it drives everything else.
**Interested in:** specific property or area.

**Property details:** unit numbers, rents, specials, fees. Copy the exact figures, never approximate them, because the number quoted has to match the number on the application.

**Done:** what has already been sent to the leasing team. Income letter, client info, anything that pre-flags the client.

**[Date] sent:** what was actually said in the last message, in one or two lines.

**Next:** the single next action. Not a list.

---

## Rules

- Referral credit is the whole commission. Every client puts Sophia R in the referral box, and that gets confirmed in writing before they apply.
- Send the income letter and client info to the leasing team before the client applies. It pre-flags them, speeds approval on thin credit, and protects the referral.
- Evictions and broken leases decide approval more often than a credit score does. Ask about them early.
- Update the client's entry the moment something changes, never from memory at the end of the day.
