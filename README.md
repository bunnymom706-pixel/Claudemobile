# Claudemobile

Working setup for a one-person apartment locating business in Austin, Texas.

Three skills, in order of how often they get used:

- **`focus`** — the daily driver. Names the single closest-to-money action and refuses to help build a system when the real answer is to do the work.
- **`one-person-business`** — finds the intersection of skill, interest, and market demand, then produces business models that scale without employees and a positioning statement for the strongest one.
- **`life-reset`** — a structured interview that turns scattered thinking into a vision, a goals hierarchy, a daily structure, and a first week plan.

## The app

- **`paradigm/index.html`** — The Impression Engine. A single-file app for changing one paradigm deliberately, built on Bob Proctor's mechanism, Price Pritchett's `You²`, and `Change Your Paradigm, Change Your Life`. Open it in a browser, no build step. Details in `paradigm/README.md`.

Operating principles live in `CLAUDE.md`. Private numbers and personal context live in `context.local.md`, which is gitignored.
