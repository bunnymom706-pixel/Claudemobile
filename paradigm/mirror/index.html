Paradigm Mirror

2 minutes

You are not choosing most of this.

A paradigm is a bundle of beliefs and habits sitting in your subconscious. Most of it went in before you were seven, absorbed from the people around you — you did not pick it and you were never asked. It then decides roughly 95% of what you do, silently, all day.

That is why willpower keeps losing. You are not arguing with a decision. You are arguing with a default.

You cannot change what you cannot see, which is the only thing this app does. Twenty-four statements, about two minutes. Then it shows you which paradigms are running you, what each one actually costs you, the exact moment you can catch it, and the one specific move that changes it.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Everything stays on this device. Answer fast and honestly — your first reaction is the accurate one, and the polite answer is not the one running you.
