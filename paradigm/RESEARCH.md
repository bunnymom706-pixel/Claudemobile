# Research behind The Impression Engine

Every design decision in this app traces to something below. Where the source material and the evidence disagree, the disagreement is named rather than smoothed over.

## 1. Bob Proctor — the mechanism

**Paradigm.** A collection of subconscious habits and beliefs, inherited from family and environment, that dictate thought, feeling and behaviour without conscious awareness. Proctor put the bulk of installation between birth and about age seven and credited paradigms with roughly 95% of behaviour.

**Results are the printout.** Whatever you are getting reads out the paradigm exactly. This makes results diagnostic rather than a verdict on effort.

**The stickperson** (originally Thurman Fleet, 1930s; the diagram Proctor called the most important thing he ever encountered). One circle for the mind, split conscious over subconscious, joined to a smaller circle for the body. The conscious mind thinks and can accept or reject. The subconscious feels and cannot reject. The body carries out orders. **Thought → feeling → action → result.**

**Two ways in.** A paradigm changes by (1) an emotional impact, or (2) the constant spaced repetition of an idea opposite to the one held. Proctor: "our paradigm was built through repetition — constant, spaced repetition — and that's the way it's going to be changed." The point of repetition is not memorisation but impression.

**Law of Vibration.** Proctor treated attraction as secondary and vibration as the primary law. Critically, **feeling is your conscious awareness of the vibration you are in** — "I don't feel well" reports a negative vibration. Awareness alone transmutes a negative vibration toward neutral, which is why the instant shift starts by naming the feeling. It is also why you do not jump from negative to positive: you pass through neutral, then choose a thought.

**Emotion is the carrier.** Thoughts alone are insufficient; feelings control vibration. An idea must be emotionalised to move into the subconscious.

**The terror barrier.** Bondage → terror barrier → freedom. A new idea drives forward while old conditioning pulls back: too scared to advance, unwilling to retreat, unable to stay. It shows up physically — cold sweat, hives, palpitations. Most people bounce back to bondage and rationalise it as comfort. Understanding what is happening is what permits the crossing.

**Six higher faculties.** Imagination, intuition, perception, memory, will, reason. Proctor's instruction was to take one at a time and work it until you see results, hence the daily rotation.

**Prosperity consciousness** (*You Were Born Rich*): wealth follows an occupied state rather than a pursued one; the Vacuum Law says release the scarcity position before expecting the new thing.

**Timeline.** Some shifts happen suddenly on emotional impact; others take weeks or months of consistent repetition. Proctor read *Think and Grow Rich* daily for over fifty years. The app's 30/60/90 milestones reflect the "weeks to months, by daily discipline" range rather than a promised number.

## 2. Napoleon Hill — the six steps Proctor taught

1. Fix the **exact amount** of money desired. Be definite.
2. Determine exactly **what you intend to give in return**.
3. Establish a **definite date**.
4. Create a plan and **begin at once, whether you are ready or not**.
5. **Write** a clear statement of amount, date, exchange and plan.
6. **Read it aloud twice daily** — once on rising, once before sleep.

Step 2 is the one users skip, so the builder makes it a required field. Step 4 is identical to Pritchett's instruction sixty years later. Step 6 sets the app's floor of two impressions a day.

## 3. Price Pritchett — *You²*

A quantum leap skips stages rather than grinding through them; explosive jumps come from a different method, not more effort. Core moves: quit trying harder, ignore conventional approaches, suspend disbelief, focus on ends over means, choose different rules, and **move before you are ready**. A clear picture of the outcome plus confident movement causes unexpected resources to materialise — "draw the map as you go." A quantum leap requires an abrupt change in behaviour, which is why the app treats the daily out-of-character act as non-optional.

## 4. Where the evidence corrects the practice

This is the part most Proctor-derived apps get wrong.

**Positive fantasy alone predicts worse outcomes.** Oettingen & Mayer (2002) found vivid pre-pursuit fantasy tied to *less* weight lost, slower recovery and lower starting salaries across four samples. The mind treats the imagined outcome as partly attained and demobilises. The correction is **mental contrasting** — picture the outcome, then immediately name the inner obstacle, in that order. So no visualization step in this app runs without an obstacle step attached.

**Implementation intentions are the strongest available lever.** Gollwitzer & Sheeran's meta-analysis (k=94) puts if-then plans at roughly **d ≈ 0.65** on goal attainment. Every habit swap in the builder is one, and the instant shift closes on one. Mental contrasting plus implementation intentions is Oettingen's **WOOP**, which is structurally what the instant shift runs.

**Affirmations can backfire.** Wood, Perunovic & Lee (2009), *Positive Self-Statements: Power for Some, Peril for Others.* Participants with low self-esteem who repeated "I'm a lovable person" felt **worse** than those who did not; only participants with high self-esteem felt better. The statement collides with the incumbent belief and loses. This is the single most important correction in the app: the builder rates believability 1–10, and anything under 5 routes to a bridge statement the person can actually accept, promoted to the real statement only once ten pieces of logged evidence support it.

**Asking beats declaring.** Senay, Albarracín & Noguchi (2010). Merely writing "Will I" rather than "I will" produced better anagram performance and stronger exercise intentions; the interrogative elicits intrinsically motivated reasons. Offered as one of the bridge forms.

**Cyclic sighing is the fastest honest route to neutral.** Balban, Huberman & Spiegel, *Cell Reports Medicine* (2023). 111 participants, five minutes daily for a month; exhale-focused cyclic sighing beat mindfulness meditation on mood improvement and respiratory rate. Two inhales through the nose, one long exhale through the mouth. It is stage two of every instant shift.

**Money scripts make Proctor's pre-seven claim measurable.** The Klontz Money Script Inventory identifies four inherited, usually unconscious beliefs formed in childhood around emotionally charged events ("financial flashpoints"): money avoidance, money worship, money status, money vigilance. Three of the four are associated with lower income, lower net worth and higher revolving credit; money vigilance is the protective one. The money roots module is eight items scoring these four.

## 5. Design consequences

| Finding | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| Repetition + emotion is the only controllable door | Spaced impressions with six rotating emotional angles |
| Feeling = awareness of vibration; go via neutral | Instant shift: name it, breathe to neutral, then choose the thought |
| Fantasy alone backfires | Every picture step is followed by an obstacle step |
| If-then plans, d≈0.65 | Habit swaps and the instant shift's closing move |
| Affirmations backfire at low self-worth | Believability rating, bridge statements, evidence-gated promotion |
| Interrogative beats declarative | "Will I…" offered as a bridge form |
| Behaviour is the faster lever | Displacement score is a geometric mean of impressions × out-of-character acts |
| Terror barrier is a boundary, not a verdict | Crossings and retreats logged without judgement |
| Exact amount, given in return, definite date | Required builder fields, divided down to a daily conversation count |

## 6. Boundary

This is belief and behaviour work. It is not treatment for trauma and does not substitute for it. The money roots module deliberately asks what **rule** was handed down and never what happened — no field exists for the event. If the childhood material itself becomes the loud part, that is a different track and it needs a qualified person.
