# Backlog

Good ideas that are not today's idea. Nothing here is rejected — it's queued.
The top of the list gets built after the build ladder in the skill runs out.

## Next up (the ladder, in order)

- **v2 — Two-minute version.** A smaller "gateway" tap next to the main one, for
  the days when the full habit isn't happening. The chain survives a bad day.
- **v3 — Habit stacking.** An anchor sentence at the top: "after I ___, I ___".
  The cue is the first thing visible when the app opens.
- **v4 — Up to three habits.** Hard cap at three. Four is a to-do list.
- **v5 — Focus block.** Name one thing, run a timer, log it against the habit.

## Parked

- Weekly / monthly view beyond the 35-day grid
- Reminders or notifications (needs more than a single HTML file)
- Sync between devices (backup/restore covers this for now)
- Stats, charts, best-streak-ever, completion percentage
- Themes and colour choices
- Notes attached to a day

## Rejected, and why

Kept here so the same idea doesn't get relitigated every session.

- **Sign-in / accounts** — fails "easy". A login between her and one tap is the
  fastest way to stop opening it.
- **A settings screen** — every setting is a decision she has to make before the
  habit happens. Settings live inline or not at all.
